## Guiding principle: generate *grayscale masters*, tint in code

Don't generate 36 colored tiles. Generate **one neutral (grayscale) tile body** with the bevel and
shadow baked in, then the engine **tints it per color** with `modulate` (a multiply). White stays
white × color = color, and the darker shading multiplies down naturally — so one asset yields all 6
colors, and adding colors later is free. Same idea for panels/frames.
